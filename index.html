The real obstacle

You may be qualified. The challenge is understanding how the Dutch hiring market evaluates that experience.

Getting hired as an expat or international, in a market where you don't have much experience, don't speak the local language, or haven't even landed yet, can be a frustrating experience. The hiring process is unfamiliar and highly competitive, and after hundreds of unanswered applications, a job interview starts to feel completely out of reach.

None of that is a verdict on your ability. It usually means your experience is being read against a set of expectations nobody explained to you.
